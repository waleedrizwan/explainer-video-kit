== seg 8
  [0] So are networks just better?
  [1] Not always.
  [2] A tree's questions work well when each input already means something on its own.
  [3] Think of a spreadsheet, with columns like hours studied, or age, or price.
  [4] Is hours studied above some cutoff, is a useful question by itself.
  [5] On data like that, trees, especially many trees combined, are often as good as a network, or better.
  [6] A single pixel isn't like that.
  [7] The meaning lives in combinations.
  [8] A stroke, a loop, a curve.
  [9] And that's exactly what hidden neurons are built to find.
  [10] Which is why networks win on raw inputs like pixels, sound, and text.
